**Simin Chun, Ph.D.** is a postdoctoral researcher with a background in pharmaceutical chemistry, lipid nanoparticle development, and mRNA delivery.

Simin received her Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Chemistry from the College of Pharmacy at Seoul National University, where she studied iron-catalyzed transfer hydrogenation of N-heterocycles. As a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Catholic University of Korea, she expanded her research into photochemistry-based ionizable lipid discovery, high-throughput LNP screening, and mRNA delivery.

Her research interests center on developing new functional materials for RNA nanomedicine through high-throughput molecular discovery, with particular interests in ionic liquids and lipid nanoparticles, as well as in understanding how molecular structure and interfacial properties influence biological transport and therapeutic performance. Outside the lab, Simin enjoys listening to music, exploring new places, and finding good coffee.
